How these apply

Every change is written to this player's save on the launcher and read by the app at its next login - back out to the title screen (or restart the app) after changing something. Each field remembers the value it had before its first change, so Restore puts the player back exactly.

Presets

Wallet and stamina

Gold (金幣), star atoms (星原子 - used to buy levels and make stars), the eight stones, and stamina (60 is the normal cap; one point returns every 10 minutes).

Games, levels and stars

Games open in the world map's order (UnlockedGame = how many are open). Each game's highest unlocked level is 1-7; a star marks its constellation as made.

Highest rank per level

What the map shows for each level, and what counts as passed. "—" keeps the rank the player actually earned (shown in grey); a choice overrides it.
